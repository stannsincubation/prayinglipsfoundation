About Praying Lips Foundation

Bridging Education, Innovation, and Social Upliftment

Praying Lips Foundation is a forward-thinking non-profit organization dedicated to fostering self-reliance, academic excellence, and humanitarian support across diverse communities.

From school-level skill development and high-end technological incubation to corporate CSR partnerships and direct animal welfare drives, we build ecosystem-wide solutions for sustainable impact.

Driven By Vision, Guided By Mission

Our Vision

To cultivate an empowered society where youth, research scholars, and vulnerable communities gain access to high-quality technical education, entrepreneurial opportunities, and compassionate social support.

Our Mission

To deliver scalable skill development workshops, facilitate science and technology transfers, incubate innovations, and execute direct grass-root social and animal welfare drives with total transparency.

What We Do

Core Programmes & Verticals

School & College Training

Micro-mentorship programs, skill development workshops, and career readiness sessions tailored for school pupils and university students.

Women Empowerment

Specialized skill-training initiatives focused on financial independence, vocational skills, and entrepreneurship support for women.

Incubation & Startup Support

Providing foundational guidance, office resources, and mentorship for early-stage social impact innovations and technological ventures.

R&D — Academic Support

Supporting college students in research and project development, equipping them with prototyping components and guidance.

Science & Tech Transfer

Accelerating knowledge transfer in cutting-edge domains like Robotics, Automation, and applied engineering solutions.

Social & Animal Welfare

Direct outreach providing grain distribution, meal drives, school supplies, and stray animal feeding & healthcare support.
